Scheduling texts – Terms and conditions

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The program

Ezer is the scheduling assistant for Jalen Garmon at One Community Church. If you agree to receive scheduling texts, Ezer may text you to offer times for a check-in, care conversation or meeting with Jalen, confirm the time you choose, and remind you on the day. Texts are only about scheduling. Ezer never sends marketing, promotional or fundraising texts.

How you agree

You agree in person or by phone when Jalen asks whether his scheduling assistant may text you about meeting times. Jalen records the date and how you agreed. Agreeing is not a condition of membership, giving, or taking part in any ministry or event.

How often

Message frequency varies, usually 1 to 5 texts each time a meeting is scheduled. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. You'll receive one text confirming you've opted out, and no more after that.
  • Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact Jalen at One Community Church directly.

Other terms

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. If you reply with anything other than scheduling, such as a prayer request or a question, Ezer passes it to Jalen personally and doesn't answer it. Your information is handled as described in the privacy policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
