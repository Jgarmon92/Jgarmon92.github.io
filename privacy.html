Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Chief of Staff is a personal tool that runs on its owner's Mac and is used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): event times, titles, locations, attendees and response status, to build a schedule and see which meetings are confirmed.
  • Gmail (read-only): message headers (from, to, date, subject) and short snippets, to see when the owner last heard from a contact.
  • Google Drive (only files this app creates): to upload and remove its own encrypted backup files. It cannot see any other files in Drive.

How it is used and stored

  • Data is stored only on the owner's Mac, in a local database readable only by the owner's account. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Email snippets are deleted after 30 days.
  • Backups uploaded to Drive are encrypted on the Mac before upload; only the owner holds the passphrase.
  • The app cannot send email, create, change or delete calendar events, or read other Drive files.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Email content is never sent to any AI service. If the owner turns on optional AI drafting, only structured facts with names, emails, companies and phone numbers replaced by anonymous IDs (for example P-0012) are sent, and every request is logged on the Mac for the owner to review.

Chief of Staff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect Google at any time in Settings, which revokes access and deletes the stored token, and can delete all local data with one command. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
