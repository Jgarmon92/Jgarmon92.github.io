Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Jalen Garmon uses a private tool, Chief of Staff, and its scheduling assistant, Ezer, to care for members of One Community Church. The tool runs only on Jalen's Mac and is used only by him. This policy explains what it handles and how it is protected.

Information from Google

  • Google Calendar: event times, titles, locations, attendees and responses, to see Jalen's availability. When a member picks a time by text, Ezer adds that one meeting to Jalen's calendar.
  • Gmail (read-only): message headers (from, to, date, subject) and short snippets, to see when Jalen last heard from someone. The tool cannot send email.
  • Google Drive (only files this tool creates): to store its own encrypted backups. It cannot see any other files in Drive.

How information is kept

  • Everything is stored only on Jalen's Mac, in a database only his account can read. Sign-in keys are kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • Prayer requests and pastoral care notes stay on the Mac. They are never texted, emailed, shared or sent to any AI service, and never appear in reports.
  • Email snippets are deleted after 30 days. Backups are encrypted on the Mac before upload, and only Jalen holds the key.

Text messages (SMS)

Ezer texts only members who have agreed, in person or by phone, to receive scheduling texts. Texts are used only to offer times for a check-in or meeting with Jalen, confirm the time chosen, and send a reminder on the day.

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
  • Phone numbers and text history are stored only on Jalen's Mac and used only for scheduling.
  • Replies are read on Jalen's Mac. They are never sent to a cloud AI service.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.

See the texting terms.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold or shared with third parties, and is never used for advertising or fundraising. If Jalen turns on optional AI help for drafting messages, only general facts with names, emails and phone numbers replaced by anonymous IDs (for example P-0012) are sent, and every request is logged for him to review. Email content, text messages, prayer requests and care notes are never sent.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP, or ask Jalen to delete your information. Jalen can disconnect Google at any time, which revokes access.

Contact

Questions: contact Jalen Garmon at One Community Church.
