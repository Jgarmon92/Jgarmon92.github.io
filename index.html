Chief of Staff

Chief of Staff is a private tool that Jalen Garmon uses on his own Mac to organize pastoral care at One Community Church: keeping up with members, scheduling check-in conversations, and making sure no one is overlooked.

It is not a public service. It is used only by Jalen, and there is no sign-up.

  • Reads Jalen's Google Calendar and Gmail to see upcoming meetings and when he last connected with someone.
  • Stores everything on Jalen's Mac. Prayer requests and care notes never leave it.
  • Uses Google Drive only to store its own encrypted backup files.
  • Its scheduling assistant, Ezer, texts members who have agreed to receive scheduling texts, to offer times for a check-in, confirm the time they choose, and send a reminder on the day.
