Chief of Staff

Chief of Staff is a private, personal productivity tool that runs on its owner's own Mac. It builds a weekly scorecard, daily checklist and meeting plan from the owner's own Google Calendar and Gmail.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, and there is no account sign-up.

  • Reads Calendar and Gmail (read-only) to see meetings and who has been in touch.
  • Stores everything on the owner's Mac.
  • Uses Google Drive only to store its own encrypted backup files.
