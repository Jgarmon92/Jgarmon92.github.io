How members agree to scheduling texts

One Community Church · Scheduling texts from Ezer, Jalen Garmon's scheduling assistant

Opt-in method: verbal, in person or by phone. No one is texted unless they have said yes to the script below and that consent has been recorded.

1. The verbal script

Jalen Garmon reads this to the member in person or by phone, and waits for a clear yes or no:

"Would it be okay if my scheduling assistant, Ezer, texted you from One Community Church's scheduling number to set up times for us to meet? You'd get a few texts each time we schedule: some time options, a confirmation, and a reminder on the day. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop the texts, or HELP for help. Our texting terms and privacy policy are on our scheduling page. Saying yes isn't required for anything at church. Is that okay with you?"
  • If the member says yes, consent is recorded (step 2). If they say no or are unsure, they are not texted.
  • Agreeing is not a condition of membership, giving, or taking part in any ministry or event.
  • Texts are only about scheduling. No marketing, promotional or fundraising texts are sent.

Texting terms: sms-terms.html · Privacy policy: privacy.html

2. How consent is recorded

Right after the member says yes, Jalen records it on the member's contact in his scheduling app: the date, and how they agreed. The app will not let Ezer text anyone without this record, and it marks the person as opted out the moment they reply STOP.

Example record (sample data).

3. The first text the member receives

The first message names the church and the assistant and repeats how to opt out:

Hi [First name], this is Ezer, Jalen's scheduling assistant at One Community Church. Jalen would like 30 minutes with you to check in. Do any of these work? 1) [Day, date, time] 2) [Day, date, time] 3) [Day, date, time] Reply 1, 2 or 3, or tell me a day and time that works better. Reply STOP to opt out. - Ezer, Jalen's assistant

4. Opting out

Replying STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT or UNSUBSCRIBE stops all texts immediately. The member receives one confirmation and nothing further. Replying START opts back in. Replying HELP returns contact information.
